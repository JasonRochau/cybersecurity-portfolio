# Professional Statement

A short statement of who I am, the strengths I bring, and what draws me to cybersecurity. It's written for cybersecurity recruiters.

## Statement

I am a highly self-motivated IT professional who takes ownership of my work and holds myself accountable to the organizations I support. My core set of strengths lie in written and verbal communications, time management, and programming with languages including Python and SQL. This positions me well to move into a security analyst role and start contributing quickly rather than needing a lot of time to acclimate. I am drawn to cybersecurity because it allows me to protect people's and organizations' data by applying the discipline I practice at home hardening and monitoring my own network. I believe security should protect people's access, not just lock systems down, and I am committed to continuous learning and to improving both my skills and the systems I protect.
